Frequently asked

Do I need programming experience?

No. A large share of our members write their first line of code at one of our workshops. Every session is labelled with a difficulty level, and the beginner ones assume nothing at all.

Do I have to be a Computer Science student?

Not at all. We have members from Management, Psychology, Biology, Media Studies, and plenty of other programs. If you're a UTSC student, you're welcome.

Is there a membership fee?

No. The club is funded by the student union and our sponsors, so events, food, and hackathon travel are free for members.

How much time does it take?

Entirely up to you. Some members come to one workshop a term; others join a project team and spend a few hours a week on it. Both are completely normal.

Can I join partway through the term?

Yes. Workshops are standalone, so you can start at any point. Project teams are easiest to join in the first three weeks, but reach out anyway — teams often need help later on.

I want to sponsor or collaborate with the club.

We'd love to hear from you. Email the exec team and we'll send over our sponsorship package.
