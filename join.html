Frequently asked

Do I need to be a physics student?

No. We have members from Mathematics, Computer Science, Chemistry, Neuroscience, Astronomy, and Philosophy. Every event is labelled by difficulty so you know what you're walking into.

How much physics do I need to know?

For most events, high-school physics is plenty. Observation nights and outreach shows need none at all. Our Advanced-labelled seminars assume second-year mechanics and E&M.

Is there a fee?

No. Membership and events are free, funded by the student union, the department, and our conference sponsors.

Can I join partway through the year?

Yes, any time. Events are standalone, so there's nothing to catch up on.

I'm an alum or a grad student. Can I still be involved?

Please do. We're always looking for seminar speakers and conference judges. Email the exec team and tell us what you work on.

Do you share my email with anyone?

No. Responses go to a form owned by the exec team, and we use them only for club communications. You can ask to be removed at any time.
