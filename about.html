About the club

Who we are

Newton's Apples Physics Society was founded in 2022 by students who wanted somewhere to argue about physics outside of a graded setting.

🎯

Our mission

To make physics at UTSC something you participate in rather than only sit through — by giving students a place to ask questions, present work, and meet the people doing research down the hall.

🚪

Who can join

Any UTSC student. We have members from Physics, Astronomy, Mathematics, Computer Science, Chemistry, Neuroscience, and Philosophy. Enthusiasm is the only prerequisite.

💸

How we're funded

Through the student union, the Department of Physical and Environmental Sciences, and conference sponsors. That's why membership and events stay free.

What we believe

Three principles that decide how we run everything.

Questions beat answers

Our seminars budget more time for questions than for slides. The point of a talk is the conversation afterwards, and nobody is made to feel slow for asking.

Undergraduates can do research

Not eventually — now. The conference exists because a second-year with a summer project deserves a real audience, not a poster board in a hallway.

Physics isn't only for physicists

Some of our most active members are in other programs entirely. We label every event by difficulty so you always know what you're walking into.

How we got here

2021

Founded

Four students, a booked classroom, and a weekly problem-solving session that eleven people showed up to.

2022

First observation night

A borrowed 8-inch reflector and forty students on the field at 10 PM in November. It has run monthly ever since.

2023

First undergraduate conference

Nine student talks over a single afternoon. We expected thirty attendees and had to move rooms twice.

2025

Outreach programme begins

Demo shows at four Scarborough high schools, reaching roughly 600 students in the first year.

2027

Conference goes two days

Our sixth conference expands to a full weekend, with a keynote and a poster session. Details here.

Frequently asked

I'm not a physics major. Will I be lost?

No. Every event is labelled Introductory, Intermediate, or Advanced. Introductory events assume first-year mechanics at most, and often less than that. Our observation nights and outreach shows assume nothing at all.

Is there a membership fee?

No. Membership, events, and conference attendance are free for UTSC students, funded by the student union and our sponsors.

How much time does it take?

Whatever you want it to. Plenty of members come to one seminar a term. Others help run the conference, which is a few hours a week in the lead-up.

Can I get involved in running things?

Yes, and we always need help. You can volunteer for a single event, join a conference committee, or run for exec in the March elections. See the team page.

Still curious?

Membership is free, takes two minutes, and commits you to precisely nothing.
