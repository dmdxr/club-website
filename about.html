About the club

Newton's Apples Physics Society is the physics club at the University of Toronto Scarborough. We run workshops, talks, and a good deal of fun — movie nights, Choose Your Program Night, and our own undergraduate conference. You don't need to be a physics major. You just need to be curious.

What we get up to

Some of it is physics. Some of it is pizza and a projector. All of it is free and open to every UTSC student.

🔬

Workshops

Hands-on sessions where you build something and take it home — from classifying galaxies with machine learning to mainframe computing with IBM Z.

🎬

Movie nights

A film, a room full of friends, and snacks. Our favourite way to destress — and reliably the event people ask us to run again.

🧭

Choose Your Program Night

Third and fourth-years answer the question every first-year is quietly worrying about: which physics program, and what is it actually like?

🍎

Our own conference

NAUPC, the Newton's Apples Undergraduate Physics Conference. Present your research, or just come along and listen to your classmates present theirs.

🎤

Guest speaking seminars

Researchers, alumni, and people working in industry come in to talk about what they actually do — pitched at undergraduates, not at their peers.

🥤

Social & networking mixers

Meet the rest of the club, and the people a few years ahead of you. Low-key, no name tags, and a good place to find a study group or a summer supervisor.

🧪

Lab tours

Behind-the-door visits to research groups on campus, to see the equipment and the people using it up close.

🔭

Outreach

Demo shows for high-school students, run by our members. No physics background required — enthusiasm carries the day.

Questions people actually ask

Do I have to be a physics student?

No. We're open to every student at UTSC, in every program and every year. Plenty of our regulars are here because a movie night looked fun and they stayed for the rest.

Does it cost anything?

No. Our events are free and open to all UTSC students, and none of them need advance registration — you can just turn up.

How do I find out when things are happening?

Everything lands on our events page first. Instagram carries last-minute changes, like a room switch or a clouded-out observation night.

Can I help run things?

Yes, and you don't need a title to do it. Members help run outreach shows, sit on the conference committee, and pitch ideas for events they want to see. Email us at utsc.naps@seds.ca and we'll find you something.

Come to one thing

That's genuinely all it takes. Turn up to whatever looks interesting, and see if we're your kind of people.
