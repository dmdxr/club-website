What we do

Four things, every semester, run by students for students.

🛠️

Weekly workshops

Hands-on sessions on Git, React, Python, cloud deployment, and whatever the community asks for. Beginners genuinely welcome.

🚀

Project teams

Join a small team each term and ship something real — an app, a tool, a bot. Every project ends with a demo night.

🏆

Hackathons

We run an internal hackathon each term and travel as a group to bigger ones. We'll help you find a team.

💼

Career prep

Resume reviews, mock interviews, and alumni talks from people who were sitting in your seat two years ago.

🤝

Study jams

Open co-working sessions before midterms. Bring your laptop, bring your problem set, we'll figure it out together.

🌐

Open source

Guided first contributions to real repositories, so your GitHub has something on it before recruiting season.
