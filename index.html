What we do

Six things, every year, organised entirely by students.

🔭

Observation nights

Monthly telescope sessions on the field behind campus. We supply the optics and the star charts; you supply a warm jacket.

🎓

Seminar series

Faculty, grad students, and visiting researchers explain what they actually work on — pitched at undergraduates, not at their peers.

✏️

Problem-solving jams

Weekly sessions where we work through mechanics, E&M, and quantum problem sets together. Bring the question that's stuck.

🧪

Lab tours

Behind-the-door visits to research groups on campus. See a dilution refrigerator, a clean room, and an optics bench in person.

🎤

Undergraduate conference

Our flagship event. Undergraduates present their own research to a real audience — for many, their first ever talk.

🌍

Outreach

Demo shows at local high schools. Liquid nitrogen, Van de Graaff generators, and an audience that has never seen either.
