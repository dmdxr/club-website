What it is

A conference run by undergraduates, for undergraduates — with the format of a real one.

🎤

Give a talk

Twelve-minute talks with three minutes of questions. Summer research, a course project, or a thesis chapter all qualify.

📊

Present a poster

A lower-pressure option and a good first outing. Posters stay up both days, with a dedicated session on Saturday afternoon.

👂

Just attend

Most people do. Come for the talks that interest you, skip the rest, and stay for the food. No presentation required.

Tracks

Talks are grouped into four parallel streams.

🌌 Astrophysics & Cosmology

Observation, stellar structure, large-scale structure, and instrumentation.

⚛️ Quantum & Condensed Matter

Materials, low-temperature physics, quantum information, and computation.

🧬 Biophysics & Medical

Soft matter, imaging, biomechanics, and physics applied to living systems.

🧮 Theory & Computation

Mathematical physics, simulation, numerical methods, and modelling.

Conference FAQ

Can I attend if I'm not from UTSC?

Yes. Undergraduates from any university are welcome to attend and to submit an abstract. There is no registration fee either way.

What counts as research I could present?

More than you'd think. A summer research position, an NSERC USRA project, a fourth-year thesis, an independent study, or a substantial course project all qualify. If you're unsure, email us and describe it — the answer is usually yes.

I've never given a talk before.

That's who this is for. We run two practice sessions in February where you present to a small friendly group and get feedback before the real thing.

Is there funding for travel?

We have a small travel bursary for out-of-town presenters. Indicate your interest on the registration form and we'll follow up.

Are the talks recorded?

Only with each speaker's consent, and the form asks. Recorded talks go up on our channel about a month afterwards.

We'd like to sponsor the conference.

We'd be glad to talk. Email the exec team and we'll send the sponsorship package with tiers and past attendance figures.
