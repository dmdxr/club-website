How to get involved

You don't need a title to help run things.

🎤

Give a seminar

Read something interesting, or done a summer project? Pitch a talk. We'll help you prepare it and handle the logistics.

📋

Join a conference committee

Abstract review, logistics, sponsorship, or day-of volunteering. A few hours a week from January to March.

🔭

Help at outreach shows

Come along to a high-school demo show. No physics background required — enthusiasm carries the day.

Questions for the team?

Email us or drop a message in the Discord — someone from exec checks both daily.
