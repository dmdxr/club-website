Open position

We elect the next exec team every March, and first-years are encouraged to run. Ask any of us about it.

How to get involved

You don't need a title to help run things.

🎤

Run a workshop

Know something the rest of us don't? Pitch a session. We'll help you prep the material and handle the logistics.

🧭

Mentor a team

Upper-years can mentor a project team for a term. It's about two hours a week and it looks great on a resume.

🗳️

Run for exec

Elections happen every March and are open to any member. Most of the current team started as first-year volunteers.

Questions for the team?

Email us or drop a message in the Discord — someone from exec checks both daily.
