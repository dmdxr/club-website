How project teams work

1️⃣

Pitch or pick

At kickoff night, anyone can pitch an idea. You either join one you like or bring your own.

2️⃣

Build weekly

Teams meet once a week for about two hours. A mentor from the exec team checks in and unblocks you.

3️⃣

Demo it

End of term, you present to the club. It ships publicly, and it goes on your resume with a real link.
